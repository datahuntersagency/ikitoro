The house粋

Iki is the Japanese ideal of refinement without excess. Nothing on the plate that does not need to be there, nothing in the room that distracts from it.

Our fish is flown in daily and cut to order at the counter. Our robata is fed with charcoal and patience. Behind the bar sits one of Southern California's deepest collections of Japanese whisky and sake, poured by people who know every bottle by name.

Over a hundred years of combined mastery, one quiet room on Brand Boulevard.

Sunset Omakase

One hour, every evening, as the sun goes down. You take a seat at the counter, leave the choices to the chef, and the evening unfolds piece by piece.

Daily · 5:00 to 6:00 pm

The chef decides. You simply arrive.

The menu follows the morning's catch and the season. Seating is limited to the counter, so every guest is served directly by the chef.

Ask about a sake or Japanese whisky pairing from the cellar, or a premium sake flight. Please share allergies when you book.
